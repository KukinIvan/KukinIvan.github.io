Support

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Trial and lifetime purchase

The complete app is free to try for 30 days after the free trial is activated. The trial does not renew and never causes an automatic payment. To keep adding or editing records after it expires, buy the lifetime unlock once. The price shown in MyPlanner comes from the App Store for your country or region.

Where are my records?

Your records are stored on your iPhone, not on a MyPlanner account or cloud service. You can export a JSON backup in Settings. MyPlanner does not currently synchronize records between devices.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the MyPlanner access screen and choose Restore Purchases. Sign in with the Apple Account used to buy the full version. Trial and lifetime purchase entitlements are processed by Apple. If StoreKit is temporarily unavailable, check your connection and try again.

What happens after the trial?

You can still view events, tasks and habits and export a backup. Creating new entries, editing and marking habits complete require the one-time full unlock. Existing records are not deleted because the trial expired.

Apple Health and calendars

These connections are optional and can be managed in MyPlanner Settings or iOS system settings. Apple Health is read-only for the selected metrics, and iPhone calendar events are displayed read-only.
