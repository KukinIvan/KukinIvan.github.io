Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Your planner data

Events, lists, tasks, habits, completion history, tags, templates, recently deleted entries and Focus timer state are stored locally on your iPhone. You do not need to register an account. MyPlanner does not run a developer-hosted cloud database or cross-device synchronization. The developer does not receive the contents of your planner through this app.

2. Device permissions

Apple Health. With your permission, MyPlanner reads step counts, Apple exercise minutes or completed workouts for the habit goals you configure. These readings remain on your device and raw Health samples are not included in backups or sent to the developer. MyPlanner does not write to Apple Health.
iPhone Calendars. You can display selected device calendars on a read-only basis; MyPlanner does not modify events in them.
Notifications and Siri. MyPlanner uses iOS system frameworks for reminders and voice commands. Reminder text may appear on your Lock Screen according to iOS settings.

3. Trial and purchases

The 30-day trial and one-time full unlock use Apple's StoreKit. The App Store handles payment processing and any refunds. MyPlanner receives Apple-verified entitlement and purchase-date information for access control, but does not receive payment card details. There is no auto-renewing subscription.

4. Backups and sharing

You may export a JSON backup that contains your planner records and history. You choose where it is saved and whether to share it. If you use a third-party cloud service or app, its own privacy rules apply. The developer does not receive your backups.

5. Data retention

You may delete your entries in the app. Deleted tasks and lists may remain in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes its local records, but does not remove backup files you saved elsewhere. Apple retains App Store purchase records under Apple's terms.

6. Advertising and diagnostics

MyPlanner has no advertising trackers, third-party analytics SDK, or developer server collecting your planner data. Apple's App Store and iOS diagnostic services operate under Apple's policies.

7. Questions and changes

For help or privacy questions, email myplanner.app.support@gmail.com. You can also visit the MyPlanner support page. This policy may change as the app develops. The current version is accessible from within MyPlanner and from the App Store listing.
